The founders

A husband and wife studio, built on referrals.

Jenny and Ante Matek lead AM Bathrooms + Projects, a boutique design-led renovation studio based in Drummoyne, Sydney.

With over two decades of experience, they specialise in design-led renovations that balance thoughtful design, practical functionality and high-quality craftsmanship.

Jenny is an award-winning Interior Designer with a strong construction background. She leads the design and overall project experience, ensuring every detail is thoughtfully resolved and delivered as a cohesive, seamless renovation.

Ante (AM) has over 30 years of experience delivering exceptional craftsmanship with a meticulous, detail-driven approach. He oversees every stage of construction, ensuring the build is executed seamlessly and to the highest standard.

As a boutique team, your renovation is managed end-to-end with clear communication, attention to detail and genuine care for your home.

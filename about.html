The Founders

A husband & wife studio, built on trust.

Ante and Jenny Matek are the faces behind AM Bathrooms + Projects. Together, they bring over 45 years of combined residential building experience.

Ante is a fully licensed wall and floor tiler, waterproofer, and builder with more than 30 years of hands-on site management experience. Jenny is a qualified interior designer and project coordinator, recognized as the winner of the HIA Australian Small Business Management Award.

For decades, they have built a reputation in Sydney solely on referrals and client recommendations. Their core values are simple: best-in-class communication, meticulous tiling work, and beautiful residential finishes that last.

Based in Drummoyne, they coordinate residential projects across Sydney's Inner West, Eastern Suburbs, and Northern Beaches.
